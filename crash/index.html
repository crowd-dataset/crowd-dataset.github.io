CROWD Crash

Crashes, one clip at a time

Crash compilations from YouTube, separated into the complete source clips they were edited from. Only clips with a real crash or near collision are kept, each described with fields from a standard crash taxonomy and placed on the map only on explicit evidence.

                              Collisions

                              Kinds of collision

                              Each clip is described twice: with a plain crash type, and with the manner of collision, first harmful event and sequence of events defined by the NHTSA MMUCC guideline (6th edition, 2024). The labels come from separate automatic passes and do not always agree.

                                        Method

                                        From compilation to clip

                                        A full segment is the complete source recording between two verified edits, not just the seconds around the impact.

                                        1. Discover. YouTube searches for crash and dashcam compilations record new candidates.
                                        2. Filter. A Qwen language model rejects games, simulations, films, crash tests and motorsport before download.
                                        3. Propose cuts. FFmpeg standard and adaptive scene detectors propose where the compilation is edited.
                                        4. Verify cuts. NVIDIA Cosmos 3 Nano confirms each real edit, rejecting camera motion, impact shake, occlusion and exposure changes.
                                        5. Review clips. Cosmos reviews frames spread across each complete clip and keeps those with a real crash or near collision.
                                        6. Locate. A place is recorded only when the metadata or readable text in the video explicitly supports it.
                                        Data

                                        What each clip records

                                        One row per accepted clip in the clip table; the full model responses are kept for audit.

                                        • ClipVideo ID, stable clip ID, and the start, impact and end times
                                        • EventCrash or near collision, crash type, and MMUCC manner of collision and first harmful event
                                        • SceneCamera view, road environment, time of day, weather and road condition
                                        • Road usersHow many are visible and of which kinds
                                        • OutcomeVisible outcomes only, with a short factual description and a confidence score
                                        • PlaceLocality, state, country, continent and coordinates, with the evidence behind them

                                        Handle with care. Crash footage can be distressing and may show identifiable people. The data is shared as video identifiers, times and labels; use it in line with YouTube's terms, copyright, research-ethics approval and privacy law, and do not publish identifiable frames.
