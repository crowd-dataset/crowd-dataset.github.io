                  Method

                  How a walk becomes a segment

                  An automated pipeline finds, reviews and cuts the footage; every accepted segment carries the evidence behind it.

                  1. Discover. YouTube searches for long walking videos record new candidates, page by page, without repeats.
                  2. Filter. A Qwen language model rejects clearly unsuitable titles and descriptions before anything is downloaded.
                  3. Download. Videos are written to a temporary folder, validated and only then moved into place.
                  4. Cut. Intro detection finds where the walk starts; FFmpeg scene detection on the GPU proposes cuts.
                  5. Review. NVIDIA Cosmos 3 Nano checks each stretch for genuine walking and removes drone and other non-walking footage.
                  6. Locate. A place is recorded only from author timestamps and chapters or readable text in the video.

                  Kept

                  • Genuine walking from a pedestrian, first-person perspective
                  • Street walks, parks, markets, squares and waterfronts
                  • Indoor public areas such as stations and malls

                  Left out

                  • Drone and other aerial footage
                  • Static shots and slideshows
                  • Driving, cycling, boating and other vehicle-only movement
                  • Promotional montages, maps, title cards and introductions
                  • Segments with too little walking or low confidence
                  Data

                  What each locality records

                  One row per locality in the locality table, with list fields per video and per segment.

                  • PlaceLocality, alternative names, state, country, ISO3 code, continent and coordinates
                  • FootageYouTube video IDs, upload dates and channel IDs
                  • SegmentsStart and end time of every walking segment, in seconds
                  • Time of dayDay, night or dawn and dusk, per segment
                  • EnvironmentStreet, market, park, square, waterfront, indoor and more, per segment
                  • EvidenceAuthor timestamp labels, text read from the video, and which of the two placed it
